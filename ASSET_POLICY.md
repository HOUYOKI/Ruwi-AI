# Ruwi Exhibition Asset Policy

This build intentionally excludes the five placeholder assets that were present in the previous package (IDs 21–25). They were simple geometric placeholders and were not suitable for a museum exhibition.

The remaining 20 artifact images/models are retained as the current project reference assets. They must not be described as official museum scans unless the museum provides or approves the corresponding files.

The Saudi National Museum official site provides a public collections/galleries catalogue and states that photography/publication of museum materials is subject to its media permissions. For an exhibition deployment, replace any unverified reference asset with museum-approved high-resolution photography and, where available, museum-approved 3D/photogrammetry assets.
