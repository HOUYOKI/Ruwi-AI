# 🏛️ Ruwi | رُوي

> **Every Artifact Has a Story. Ruwi Brings It to Life.**

Ruwi is an AI-powered interactive museum platform that transforms traditional museum visits into immersive, personalized learning experiences.

Instead of relying solely on static information panels, visitors simply capture or upload an image of a museum artifact. Ruwi analyzes the artifact using Vision AI, retrieves verified historical knowledge through Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and generates an interactive educational experience tailored to that specific artifact.

Whether the artifact is an ancient tool, historical manuscript, traditional musical instrument, painting, archaeological object, or cultural item, Ruwi transforms history into an engaging digital experience through storytelling, timelines, narration, quizzes, and interactive visualizations.

---

# 📖 About

**Ruwi (رُوي)** is inspired by the Arabic verb **"روى"**, meaning **"to narrate"** or **"to tell a story."**

Every artifact carries a story that reflects the identity, culture, and history of its people. Rather than replacing museums or curators, Ruwi enhances the visitor experience by making historical knowledge more engaging, interactive, and accessible through Artificial Intelligence.

---

# 🚩 The Problem

Many museums still rely on short text descriptions or traditional audio guides that provide only basic information about artifacts.

These methods are valuable but often:

- provide the same experience to every visitor
- offer limited interaction
- cannot adapt to different ages or knowledge levels
- make it difficult for visitors to explore artifacts beyond the information on display

As a result, many visitors leave without fully understanding the significance or story behind what they saw.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

Ruwi transforms every artifact into an AI-powered interactive learning experience.

By combining **Computer Vision**, **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, **Large Language Models**, and **reusable interactive templates**, the platform generates educational experiences grounded in trusted museum knowledge rather than relying solely on AI-generated responses.

Depending on the artifact, the platform can generate experiences such as:

- 📖 Interactive storytelling
- 🗺️ Historical timelines
- 🎧 Audio narration
- 🧩 Educational quizzes
- 🔍 Clickable hotspot exploration
- ⚙️ Tool usage demonstrations
- 🎼 Traditional instrument exploration
- 🏺 Interactive visualizations
