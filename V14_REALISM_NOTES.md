# Ruwi v14 — realism and provenance notes

## What changed
- The interactive world no longer loads the museum artifact model inside the experience. The artifact remains in the separate Artifact Viewer.
- The experience is now a full scene/diorama with a large environment, contextual architecture, work surfaces, lighting, shadows, particles and direct-touch interactions.
- Scene backplates use Poly Haven CC0 preview imagery where available. They are used only as environmental backplates; the museum artifact is not represented by generated imagery.
- The app remains Arabic-first and touch-first.

## Important provenance rule
The current repository's 20 GLB files are low-poly reconstructions (the original v13 files contain only tens of vertices per model). They are NOT official photogrammetry scans from the Saudi National Museum. This version therefore does not present those reconstructions as official museum scans.

For a production exhibition, replace each artifact GLB/image pair with museum-approved digitization or official media. The Saudi National Museum's official collections page and virtual-tour announcement document its collections and 3D digital experience, while its terms require approval for photography/videography and publication.
