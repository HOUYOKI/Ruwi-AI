## 📖 Overview

**Ruwi (رُوي)** is an interactive museum-experience MVP designed for the Saudi National Museum.

Ruwi transforms traditional artifact exploration into an interactive and personalized digital experience. Visitors can browse a collection of **102 museum artifacts**, explore curated interactive experiences, upload or capture an artifact image, and ask Ruwi questions through an AI-powered narrator.

The platform combines structured museum knowledge, interactive storytelling, constrained vision identification, conversational AI, reflection metadata, and optional voice narration.

The name **Ruwi (رُوي)** is inspired by the Arabic verb **"روى"**, meaning *to narrate* or *to tell a story*.

> **Every artifact has a story. Ruwi brings it to life.**

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Traditional museum experiences often rely on static descriptions or conventional audio guides.

While these approaches provide valuable information, they can:

- Provide the same experience to every visitor
- Offer limited interaction
- Make deeper exploration difficult
- Provide limited personalization
- Separate visitors from the story behind an artifact

Ruwi aims to make museum exploration more interactive by allowing visitors to **discover, ask, explore, and learn** rather than simply read.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Ruwi turns museum artifacts into interactive digital experiences by combining structured museum content with AI-powered interaction.

Visitors can:

- 📚 Browse **102 museum artifacts**
- 🏺 Explore curated artifact experiences
- 📸 Upload or capture an artifact image
- 🤖 Identify supported artifacts using Vision AI
- 📖 Explore structured stories and historical information
- 🔍 Interact with artifact hotspots
- 🧩 Answer educational quizzes
- 🕰️ Explore historical timelines
- 🔊 Listen to AI-generated narration
- 💬 Ask Ruwi follow-up questions
- 🌐 Switch between Arabic and English
- 🌙 Use light or dark themes
- 📱 Interact through a touch-friendly interface
